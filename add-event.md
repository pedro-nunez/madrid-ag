What kind of event would you like to suggest?

## Meeting

Conferences, workshops, minicourses, summer schools, and similar programs.

[Suggest a Meeting](https://github.com/pedro-nunez/madrid-ag/issues/new?template=add-meeting.yml){: .button }

## Talk

Individual seminar talks, colloquium talks, and other standalone presentations.
Talks can belong to a recurring seminar or be standalone events.

[Suggest a Talk](https://github.com/pedro-nunez/madrid-ag/issues/new?template=add-talk.yml){: .button }

Each button opens a suggestion form on GitHub. A maintainer will review
your suggestion before adding it to the website.
All events are assumed to take place in Madrid; the forms only ask for
the hosting or organizing institution.
